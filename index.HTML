Proprietary Technology

Four Pillars of the BizMatch Engine

Replacing intuition with data. Our ecosystem is driven by advanced algorithms, NLP, and structured legal frameworks to ensure high-intent, synergistic connections.

Smart Matching: Co-Founder Finder

A multi-layered algorithm analyzing not just interests, but complementary skills and personality profiles. We scan databases to identify synergistic connections between tech and business founders—building the ultimate 'Dream Team' before the first line of code is written.

Data-Driven Investor Fit

A two-sided matching engine crossing the startup's DNA with the investor's core strategy (stage, sector, round). Features our proprietary In-App NDA layer, exposing sensitive data only upon mutual consent—making fundraising fast, secure, and fully discreet.

AI Due Diligence

One-click automated NLP (Natural Language Processing) scanning of company documents, IP, and business plans. The system generates objective risk/reward reports, identifies data gaps, and cross-references external sources which drastically reduces the investor's Time-to-Check.

Smart Pitch Prep Mentor

Our AI analyzes your Pitch Deck to identify narrative or data weaknesses, generates questions investors are highly likely to ask, and suggests real-time content improvements. This is so that the planner can arrive at the meeting ready for any scenario.
